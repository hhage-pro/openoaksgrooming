About Open Oaks

A calm groom, in your own driveway.

Open Oaks brings experienced, high-quality pet grooming to you. No car ride, no kennel wait, no crowded salon. Your pet gets one-on-one attention from a groomer who takes the time to do it right.

  • Appointments that fit your day. We schedule a window, roll up, and groom right outside your door.
  • Gentle with seniors, rescues and nervous pups. Patience first. Clients tell us their anxious dogs settle right in.
  • Owner-run by Ryan and Tara. The people you talk to are the people who groom your pet.
Services

Everything from a quick tidy to the full works.

Every pet is different, so we quote each groom individually. Tell us about your pet and we'll take it from there.

Full groom

Bath, blow-dry, brush-out and a haircut styled to your pet's breed or your preference, finished with nails and ears.

Bath & tidy

A thorough wash and dry with a light trim around the face, feet and sanitary areas. Great between full grooms.

Nail trims

Quick, low-stress trims so nails stay comfortable and your floors stay scratch-free.

Brush-outs & de-shedding

Deep brushing to lift loose undercoat and keep heavy shedders more comfortable and your house cleaner.

Seniors, rescues & matted coats

Comfort-first grooming for older dogs, anxious pups and pets who need a fresh start after matting.

Pet care while you travel

Experienced care for all kinds of pets while you're away, including giving medication and at-home health routines.
